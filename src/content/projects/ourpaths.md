## what it is

An AI-powered market-access dashboard that helps pharmaceutical teams with drug pricing and reimbursement strategy. It's a confidential industry project, so there are no screenshots, code or demo here, just the architecture.

## my role

LLM Engineer on a 5-person cross-functional team, May–Sep 2025 (UofT Real World AI/ML Systems), working with stakeholders at 3 pharmaceutical companies.

## stack

Python, GPT-4-mini, agentic RAG, a vector database, Azure Blob Storage, and the Niagara supercomputer.

## what I built

With the team, I designed and built the dashboard end to end, collaborating directly with stakeholders at three pharmaceutical companies.

I scraped and processed 800+ Health Technology Assessment (HTA) documents on the Niagara supercomputer, generating 10K+ embeddings stored in Azure Blob and a vector database for fast, scalable semantic retrieval.

On top of that, I engineered an agentic RAG pipeline with intent classification and GPT-4-mini response formatting. It routes queries correctly about 95% of the time and returns structured, domain-specific answers for industry stakeholders.
