## what it is

Socrato is an AI teaching assistant: paste in your notes and it generates summaries, quizzes and Q&A flashcards, then turns reviewing them into a small game.

## my role

Software Developer, Jan–Apr 2026.

## stack

Python, FastAPI, Gemini LLM, Pydantic.

## what I built

I built a FastAPI REST service that calls Gemini to generate summaries, quizzes and structured Q&A flashcards from a user's notes. Pydantic schema validation and robust JSON parsing keep the model's output reliable enough for production.

I also built the flashcard review UI, with flip animations, difficulty ratings and an XP and activity tracking system backed by a database, which turns spaced repetition into a gamified study session.
